Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026.

This policy describes how the personal tools referenced from this site handle data. These tools are built for the owner's personal use. They are not offered as a public service.

What data the tools access

A tool may access the owner's own account data (for example, email, calendar, photos, or files) only when the owner authorizes it through that service's sign-in / consent flow (such as Google OAuth), and only for the purpose the owner authorized. No tool on this site accesses another person's account.

How data is used

Accessed data is used solely to perform the task the owner requested (for example, reading, organizing, sending, or filing the owner's own messages, files, or photos). Data is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any other purpose.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. Data is not sent to any service other than the service the owner authorized the tool to use for that task.

Retention

Data is processed as needed to complete the owner's task and is not retained beyond that, except where the owner's own account (for example, Gmail or Google Photos) stores it as part of its normal operation.

Security

Access is granted through the account provider's authorization flow (such as Google OAuth). The owner can revoke a tool's access at any time through the account provider's security settings (for Google: Google Account > Security > Third-party access).

Children

These tools are not directed at children and do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the current version.
