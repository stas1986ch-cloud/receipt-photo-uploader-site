Receipt Photo Uploader

Receipt Photo Uploader is a personal Android app that uploads receipt photos from your phone to a private “Receipt Uploader” album in your own Google Photos library, so receipts are collected automatically for expense tracking.

How it works

  1. Install the app and sign in with Google to authorize access to your Google Photos library.
  2. Take photos of your receipts, or pick them from your gallery.
  3. Tap Upload now. The selected photos are uploaded to the “Receipt Uploader” album in your Google Photos account.

Each time the app runs, it also uploads a small run-log image summarizing the run, so you can see the app ran. Run-log images are labeled “Run log — not a receipt” and are never treated as receipts.
