Privacy Policy — Receipt Photo Uploader

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This policy describes what the Receipt Photo Uploader app accesses and how it uses it.

What the app accesses

  • Your Google account, when you sign in with Google, to authorize the app to add photos to your Google Photos library.
  • Photos you select — receipt photos you choose from your phone’s gallery.
  • Sign-in tokens, stored on your device, so the app can keep working without asking you to sign in every time.

How your data is used

The app uploads only the photos you select to the “Receipt Uploader” album in your own Google Photos account. Each run also uploads a small run-log image summarizing the run to the same album. Your photos go only to your Google Photos library — nowhere else.

The app has no ads, no analytics, and does not sell or share your data with any third party.

Retention

Uploaded photos live in your Google Photos account under your control, and you can delete them there at any time. Sign-in tokens stay on your device until you sign out, uninstall the app, or revoke access.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app’s access at any time from your Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or by uninstalling the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
